About The Cheesecake Factory WiFi

The Cheesecake Factory provides complimentary WiFi at all restaurant locations. Browse our 250+ item menu, manage your reservation, and stay connected throughout your dining experience.

Sign in with your account to access exclusive loyalty rewards, birthday perks, and seasonal menu previews while connected to our network.

Help & Support

Can't connect?
Make sure you're connected to CheesecakeFactory_WiFi in your WiFi settings, then sign out and back in to refresh your session.
How long does my session last?
Sessions are active during your dining visit.
Is my connection secure?
Your session is encrypted. As with any public network, avoid sensitive transactions like online banking while connected.

Ask a team member or visit thecheesecakefactory.com/help for assistance.
